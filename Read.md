\# Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard



\## Business Question



\*\*Where are we making and losing money, and what should the business change?\*\*



\## Project Overview



This project analyzes retail sales data using Power BI to understand sales performance, profitability, regional trends, and product-level performance. The interactive dashboard helps identify profitable and loss-making sub-categories and supports data-driven business recommendations.



\## Business Objective



\- Monitor overall revenue and profit performance.

\- Analyze monthly revenue trends.

\- Compare sales and profitability across categories and sub-categories.

\- Evaluate regional performance.

\- Identify loss-making products and areas requiring further investigation.



\## Tools and Technologies



\- \*\*Power BI Desktop\*\* — Dashboard development and data visualization

\- \*\*Power Query\*\* — Data cleaning and transformation

\- \*\*DAX\*\* — Measures and time-based calculations

\- \*\*Excel/CSV\*\* — Source dataset



\## Dataset



The project uses the Sample Superstore retail dataset, containing 9,994 transaction-level rows with information about orders, customers, products, sales, discounts, quantities, and profit.



Key fields include Order Date, Region, Category, Sub-Category, Sales, Quantity, Discount, and Profit.

\## Key Business Insights



\- \*\*Loss-making sub-categories:\*\* Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies show negative total profit despite generating sales.

\- \*\*Strong profit performers:\*\* Accessories, Phones, and Copiers are among the strongest profit-performing sub-categories in the analysis.

\- \*\*Discount analysis:\*\* Average discounts alone do not explain the observed differences in profitability.

\- \*\*Business implication:\*\* Loss-making products require further investigation into product costs, pricing, shipping expenses, and discount patterns.



\## Recommendations



1\. Investigate the cost structure and pricing of loss-making sub-categories.

2\. Review shipping expenses and discount patterns before changing product or pricing policies.

3\. Study the factors contributing to strong profitability in Accessories, Phones, and Copiers.

4\. Monitor revenue and profit trends regularly to support business decisions.
